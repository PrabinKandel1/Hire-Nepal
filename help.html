Frequently Asked Questions

What is HireNepal?
HireNepal is a platform that connects you with skilled local professionals in Pokhara. Whether you need a web developer, plumber, tutor, or any other service, you can find trusted local experts here.
Is HireNepal free to use?
Yes! Creating an account, browsing professionals, and posting jobs is completely free. You only pay the professional directly for their services.
How do I create an account?
Click on "Login" in the navigation menu, then select "Create Account". Fill in your details and you're ready to go!
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Since we're currently in demo mode, you can create a new account with a different username. In the future, we'll add password recovery via email.
How do I hire someone?
Browse professionals on the "Find Talent" page. When you find someone you like, click "View Profile" then "Hire / Contact Now" to message them directly via WhatsApp.
What is "My Hires"?
"My Hires" is where all professionals you've contacted are saved. You can message them again, view their profile, or remove them from your list.
How much do professionals charge?
Each professional sets their own rates, which are displayed on their profile. Rates in Pokhara typically range from Rs. 400 to Rs. 2000 per hour depending on the service.
How do I pay?
Payment is handled directly between you and the professional. We recommend agreeing on the price and payment method before work begins. Common options include cash, eSewa, Khalti, or bank transfer.
Is there any commission fee?
No! HireNepal is completely free. There are no commission fees or hidden charges. You pay the professional directly.
